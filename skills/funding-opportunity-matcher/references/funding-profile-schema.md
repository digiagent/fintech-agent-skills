# Funding Profile Schema

## Company and product

- Project name
- One-sentence product description
- Problem and target customer
- Business model
- Primary geography
- Incorporation and legal structure, if known
- Product stage
- Technical stack or differentiated infrastructure
- Relevant ecosystems, networks, protocols, and communities

## Capital request

- Target amount
- Minimum viable amount
- Preferred instrument: grant, SAFE, equity, token warrant, pilot revenue, sponsorship, or other
- Dilution tolerance
- Runway target
- Primary use of funds
- Deadline or urgency

## Evidence

- Product URL or demo
- MVP, prototype, or production status
- Users, revenue, pilots, LOIs, or retention
- Open-source repository and activity
- Partnerships or integrations
- Team credibility
- Hackathon, grant, award, or accelerator history
- Measurable impact evidence, if applicable

## Constraints

- Jurisdiction restrictions
- Excluded funders or ethical constraints
- Token or custody constraints
- Application bandwidth
- Required check size
- Required speed to close
