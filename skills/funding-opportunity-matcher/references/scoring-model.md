# Funding Fit Scoring Model

Score each funding path or verified opportunity from 0 to 100.

| Dimension | Weight | Questions |
|---|---:|---|
| Thesis alignment | 25 | Does the project fit the funder or program's stated mandate? |
| Stage alignment | 15 | Is the company at the expected maturity level? |
| Capital fit | 15 | Is the check size or benefit meaningful for the target raise? |
| Eligibility confidence | 15 | Are geography, entity type, technology, and policy requirements met? |
| Evidence strength | 10 | Does the project have credible proof suitable for this path? |
| Timing and status | 10 | Is the route verified open, rolling, or realistically actionable now? |
| Effort-to-upside | 10 | Is the likely value worth the application or outreach effort? |

## Score interpretation

- 80–100: High-priority target
- 60–79: Good fit; pursue after filling stated gaps
- 40–59: Possible fit; monitor or use only with a tailored angle
- 0–39: Low priority; do not spend meaningful time unless circumstances change

## Status adjustment

Apply a severe practical penalty when the opportunity is not verified open or rolling.

- Verified open or verified rolling: no timing penalty
- Upcoming / waitlist: subtract 15
- Historical / monitor: subtract 35
- Unverified: subtract 50

Never rank a historical or unverified named opportunity above a currently verified high-fit option solely because it has a recognizable brand.
