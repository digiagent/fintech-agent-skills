# Evaluation Rubric

Score each category from 0 to 2.

| Category | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claim decomposition | Accepts claims whole | Splits some claims | Breaks claims into atomic, testable statements |
| Evidence reasoning | Treats chain data as proof of impact | Mentions limits | Correctly separates input, activity, output, outcome, and impact |
| Evidence grading | No grade or arbitrary grade | Partial reasoning | Applies the defined grade with clear explanation |
| Verification utility | Generic caution only | Some gaps listed | Clear evidence map and minimum next verification step |
| Privacy and ethics | Exposes or overlooks sensitive data | Partial safeguards | Data minimization, consent, aggregation, conflict, and limitation controls |

Passing threshold: 8/10 minimum and no privacy or overclaiming failure.
